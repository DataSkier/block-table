Block Table

Fill the outlined shapes at the table's edge with pattern blocks. Finish one and a sea creature swims away into the reef.

Set up

  1. Mirror this phone to the projector and aim the projector down at a plain, light table or floor.
  2. Prop the phone above the table so its back camera sees the whole projected picture.
  3. Clear the table and tap Start. Four dots flash while the camera lines itself up.
