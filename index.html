Play Table

Projector games you play on the table with real blocks or just your hands. A phone camera watches what you make.

Setting up the table

  1. Mirror this phone to the projector or TV and point the projector down at a plain, light table.
  2. Mount the phone above the table, sideways, with its back camera seeing the whole projected picture.
  3. Pick a game, clear the table, and tap Start with camera. Four dots flash while the camera lines itself up.
