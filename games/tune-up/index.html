Table Tune-Up

Six short scenes check how well the camera sees the table: the picture, the delay, the line-up, the colours and reading over moving pictures. Then fireflies land on your fingertips.

Set up

  1. Mirror this phone to the projector and aim the projector down at a plain, light table.
  2. Prop the phone above the table so its back camera sees the whole projected picture.
  3. Clear the table and tap Start. Four coloured corners appear: turn the phone until each one shows a tick.
  4. Then keep hands off the table for about a minute while it measures.
