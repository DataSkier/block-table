Roulette

Bet with real blue and yellow chips on the projected table, then wave a hand down over the wheel to spin it.

Set up

  1. Mirror this phone to the projector and aim the projector down at a plain, light table.
  2. Prop the phone above the table so its back camera sees the whole projected picture.
  3. Clear the table and tap Start. Four dots flash while the camera lines itself up.
  4. Blue and yellow are the two players. Put chips on numbers, on the lines between them, or on the outside bets.
  5. Wave a hand from the top of the wheel to the bottom to spin. A faster wave spins harder.
