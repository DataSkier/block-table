Shadow Puppets

Make an animal with your hands in the light. When the shadow on the stage matches, that animal's short film plays.

Set up

  1. Mirror this phone to the projector and aim it at a plain, light table or wall.
  2. Prop the phone near the projector so its back camera sees the whole projected picture.
  3. Keep hands out of the light and tap Start. Four dots flash while the camera lines itself up.
  4. Hold your hands in the beam so their shadow lands on the bright stage. The cards under the stage show the shapes.
